Lifting voices, shaping futures.

We are the sole Congressional Debate circuit in the state of Illinois, dedicated to empowering high school students from all walks of life into becoming the next generation of leaders, thinkers, advocators, and informed citizens.

About ICDA Congressional Debate Members Leadership How do I join?

"Congressional debate gave me the foundation I needed to succeed as a lawmaker. It strengthened my skills in listening, navigating complex issues, analyzing issues through the perspective of different stakeholders, and articulating my arguments in a succinct manner. But more importantly, it gave me a level of confidence and compassion that I wish for every graduating senior in our state. Congressional debate is an invaluable developmental and educational activity for the leaders of our future."

- Nabeela Syed, State Representative (51st District) & former ICDA Captain of Captains
