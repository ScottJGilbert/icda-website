Welcome to the ICDA!

The Illinois Congressional Debate Association (ICDA) provides leadership for the development, supervision, and promotion of congressional debate competition across Illinois. We stress the educational importance of congressional debate activities, along with a spirit of cooperation and friendship amongst our membership.

- Tim Waters, ICDA President
